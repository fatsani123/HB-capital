About

Markets, engineered.

I'm Fatsani — a financial engineer at heart, specializing in quantitative market analysis, derivatives pricing, and the design of trading systems. My work lives at the intersection of financial modeling and market execution: building the pricing models, risk frameworks, and automated systems that turn market theory into a tradeable edge.

That engineering discipline runs through everything I build — from macro sentiment dashboards to algorithmic strategies deployed on MetaTrader and Deriv. Underneath it sits an accounting foundation from Malawi's public finance sector, the discipline that keeps every model honest: nothing reaches a live account without being reconciled against real numbers first.

HB Capital is where that same rigor is brought to mentorship — not trading tips, but the actual engineering behind a system you can trust and run yourself.
